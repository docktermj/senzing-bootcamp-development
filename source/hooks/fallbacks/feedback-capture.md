## Feedback, notes and verbosity, at any time

Read every bootcamper message for three requests, whatever step the bootcamp is on:

- **Bootcamp feedback** ("bootcamp feedback", "I have feedback", a complaint or suggestion about
  the bootcamp itself): follow `feedback.md`, then return to where the bootcamper left off.
- **A note** ("make a note", "remind me", "note that"): follow `notes.md`, then return.
- **A verbosity change** ("be briefer", "more detail", "change verbosity"): apply the
  "Verbosity" rule in `ground-rules.md`, then return.

Each is an interruption, not a detour: the step the bootcamper was on is still the current step.
