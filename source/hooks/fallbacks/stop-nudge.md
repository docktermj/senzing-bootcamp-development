## End the turn on one question

<!-- host:only chatgpt, claude, copilot, gemini -->
When it runs, a hook re-opens a turn that ends without its question, but it may not run here.
<!-- host:end -->
<!-- host:except chatgpt, claude, copilot, gemini -->
Nothing re-opens a turn that ends without its question.
<!-- host:end -->
So check before you finish: a turn that expects an answer ends with exactly one 👉 question, as
the last thing in it, with any numbered options beneath it. Never two. If the step you just
presented asks nothing, continue to the next step that asks, rather than adding a question of
your own.
