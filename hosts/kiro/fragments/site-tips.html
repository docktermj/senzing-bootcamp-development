Model

One model is fine for the whole bootcamp. Opus 5.5 suits it well. The bootcamp never asks you to switch models or change settings along the way.

Optional hooks

Kiro does not load hooks from a Power, so the bootcamp follows its safety rules as instructions. Early on it offers to install a small set of hooks into your project's .kiro/hooks/ folder so two of those rules are enforced mechanically. It shows every file first and installs nothing without your yes.

Update or uninstall

This page has no verified steps to update or uninstall the Power. Look in Kiro’s Powers panel, where you installed it.
