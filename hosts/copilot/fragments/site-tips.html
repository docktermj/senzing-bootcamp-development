Model

One model is fine for the whole bootcamp. A high-capability model suits it well, and which models you can pick depends on your plan and client. The bootcamp never asks you to switch models or change settings along the way.

Update or uninstall

In the Copilot app, open Customize > Installed. In the Copilot CLI, update the plugin:

copilot plugin update senzing-bootcamp@senzing-bootcamp

Uninstall the plugin:

copilot plugin uninstall senzing-bootcamp@senzing-bootcamp

Remove the marketplace:

copilot plugin marketplace remove senzing-bootcamp
